Recorder captures meetings, lectures, interviews and ideas, and turns them into text you can search, without anything leaving your phone.

Record
• Standard (AAC), high quality (AAC) or lossless (WAV) audio, in mono or stereo
• Pick the microphone: phone, wired headset or USB
• Clear voice reduces background noise and levels the volume
• Pause and resume, also from the notification; recording pauses itself during calls
• Start from the Quick Settings tile, the home screen widget or a shortcut
• If the app is ever stopped mid-recording, the audio is recovered the next time you open it

Transcribe, on your phone
• Live transcript while you record, saved with the recording
• Download a speech model for your language (English, German, French, Spanish, Italian, Portuguese, Russian, Hindi, Chinese, Japanese and more); it runs offline
• Speaker labels tell voices apart, and you can name them
• Sound labels mark music, applause, laughter and more on the waveform
• Edit the text, and share it as text or subtitles (SRT, WebVTT)

Find and play
• Search every word, title, place and label across all your recordings
• Playback from 0.5× to 3×, skip silence, and jump back 5 or forward 10 seconds
• The transcript follows along: tap any line to jump to it
• Keywords and key moments help you skim long recordings
• Favourites, labels and an optional place for each recording

Edit and share
• Trim a recording or cut out a part, saved as a copy so the original stays
• Turn a moment into a video clip with the waveform and captions
• Share recordings with any app, or import audio files
• Deleted recordings stay in the trash for 30 days

Private and open
• No ads, no accounts, no tracking, nothing uploaded
• The internet is used only to download the speech models you choose, on Wi-Fi by default
• Free and open source under the Apache-2.0 licence, based on the LineageOS Recorder: github.com/PVOT-OSS/Recorder
